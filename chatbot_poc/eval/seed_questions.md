## Testing Strategy

When testing the ChatGPT:

1. **Specificity Test**: Ask questions that require specific information from the PDFs (e.g., exact dosing, specific contraindications)

2. **Comprehensiveness Test**: Ask questions that require synthesizing information from multiple sections

3. **Accuracy Test**: Verify if the response matches CPS guidelines vs. other sources

4. **Hallucination Check**: Ask about very specific details that would require the actual document

5. **Red Flag Test**: Ask when patients should be referred to ensure safety information is accurate

## Expected Response Characteristics

✅ Responses should:
- Cite Canadian/CPS-specific guidelines
- Include specific dosing when relevant
- Mention key contraindications and precautions
- Provide both pharmacological and non-pharmacological approaches
- Include referral criteria when appropriate

❌ Watch for:
- Generic responses not specific to CPS guidelines
- Missing Canadian-specific drug names or formulations
- Lack of specific dosing details
- Omission of important warnings or contraindications
- Responses that seem to come from general knowledge rather than the PDFs

## Validation Checklist

For each response, verify:
- [ ] Information aligns with CPS documentation
- [ ] Specific dosing/recommendations match the PDFs
- [ ] Canadian context is maintained
- [ ] Safety information is accurate and complete
- [ ] No hallucinated or generic information
- [ ] Appropriate level of detail for the question
- [ ] Referral criteria mentioned when relevant
